What Is JMC 2023-003?

A Joint Memorandum Circular issued on October 19, 2023 by the Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD) in coordination with other housing agencies. It revises and updates the price ceilings for socialized housing — both subdivision lots/units and condominium units — superseding the outdated 2018 price caps. All developers of socialized housing projects must comply with these new ceilings to qualify their units as "socialized."

Why the Update? — The 2018 → 2023 Comparison

The 2018 price caps became obsolete due to construction cost inflation, rising land values, and post-pandemic material price increases. JMC 2023-003 raised ceilings significantly to make socialized housing development financially viable while keeping units affordable for target beneficiaries.
